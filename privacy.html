Habitat privacy policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026.

Habitat is a personal finance app used only by its author, Franko Tujani. It has no other users.

Google Drive

On the phone, Habitat asks for access to Google Drive so that it can sync with the author's computers. It uses that access to find its own sync folder (by looking for the device.json files Habitat writes there) and to read and write the files in that folder. It does not read, change or delete any other files.

Access tokens are held in memory by Google Play services and Habitat, and are not stored by Habitat or sent anywhere other than Google's Drive API.

Data

Everything Habitat records stays on the author's own devices and in his own Google Drive. Nothing is sent to the author or to anyone else, and nothing is sold, shared or used for advertising. There are no analytics.

Revoking access

Access can be removed at any time from Habitat's "Sign out" button or from Google Account › Third-party connections.
